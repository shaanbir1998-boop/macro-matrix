What this app does + how calculations work

APP OVERVIEW

Macro Matrix is a nutrition calculator that lets you build meals like a matrix. Each ingredient acts as a vector (protein, carbs, fat), and totals are summed against your daily targets in real time.

BMR FORMULA

This app uses the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR):

Men: BMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) + 5

Women: BMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is calculated by multiplying BMR by your activity level.

MACRO LOGIC

Suggested macros are based on bodyweight:

• Protein ≈ 0.9g per lb
• Fat ≈ 0.35g per lb
• Carbs fill remaining calories
